The Fight Against Protection Rackets: Four suspects arrested for extorting protection money from a local council in the north and from wastewater treatment facilities

At the conclusion of a covert investigation by the Northern Protection Racket Division in Lahav 433, together with the "Erez" Central Investigative Unit (Yamar) of the Northern Border Police, the Northern National Guard of the Border Police, and officers of the Northern District, forces raided the homes and businesses of four suspects from the northern and southern regions of the country.

The four (in their 30s and 40s) are suspected of extorting protection money and extortion under threats from a local council in the north and from wastewater treatment facilities, which constitute national resources, and are additionally suspected of money laundering offenses and offenses against the Tax Ordinance.

During the raid, luxury vehicles, checks, stamps, invoices, and Rolex watches valued at 300,000 shekels were seized.
